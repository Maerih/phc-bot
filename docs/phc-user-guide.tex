\section{Getting started}

Every command starts with a full stop (\cmd{.}). Type it in the group, or in a private chat with the bot, and send it like a normal message.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.menu} & The main menu \\
\cmd{.help} & Every command, in a list \\
\cmd{.help scholarships} & How one command works, with examples \\
\cmd{.start} & A quick introduction \\
\cmd{.about} & About the Colosseum \\
\cmd{.ping} & Check the bot is awake \\
\cmd{.sources} & Which websites the bot searches \\
\end{commands}

\begin{tip}
\textbf{Tip:} capital letters don't matter. \cmd{.Job}, \cmd{.JOB} and \cmd{.job} all work.
\end{tip}

\section{Finding opportunities}

Add keywords and a place after any search command. Places can be a town, county, country or ``remote'', and you can name several: \cmd{.job public health nairobi, kisumu}.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.job} \textit{keywords} & Jobs by keyword. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.job community health Kisumu} \\
\cmd{.jobs} \textit{place / type / today} & Jobs by place, type or how recent. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.jobs Nairobi}, \cmd{.jobs remote}, \cmd{.jobs today} \\
\cmd{.scholarships} & Scholarships and tuition funding. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.scholarships masters public health} \\
\cmd{.internships} & Internships and attachments. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.internships nutrition nairobi} \\
\cmd{.fellowships} & Fellowships and fellow programmes. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.fellowships global health} \\
\cmd{.research} & Research posts, PhD and postdoc openings. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.research epidemiology} \\
\cmd{.grants} & Grants and calls for proposals. \newline e.g.\ \cmd{.grants africa} \\
\cmd{.events} & Events, webinars and summits \\
\cmd{.conferences} & Conferences and calls for abstracts \\
\cmd{.training} & Short courses, workshops and training \\
\end{commands}

The opportunity commands search the bot's job sources \emph{and} the web, using AI to keep only real, open opportunities that Kenyans can apply for. Every result has a link to the real listing.

\subsection{Moving through results}

Results come five at a time.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.next} & The next five results \\
\cmd{.prev} & The previous five \\
\cmd{.details 2} & The full description of result number 2 \\
\end{commands}

\section{Opportunity alerts}

Turn alerts on and the bot posts new public-health opportunities into the chat by itself, as tidy cards. Each card shows the position, organisation, location, salary, deadline, requirements, a short summary and the link to apply, on the employer's own website whenever possible. It watches more than 80 Kenyan and international job, scholarship, fellowship and volunteering sites.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.alerts on} & Start updates in this chat \\
\cmd{.alerts off} & Stop them \\
\cmd{.alerts} & Status: on or off, sites watched, how many are waiting \\
\cmd{.alerts sites} & The list of websites watched \\
\end{commands}

\textbf{When updates arrive (Nairobi time):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Monday 3:00 AM to Friday 1:00 PM} --- a new opportunity every few minutes.
  \item \textbf{Friday 2:00 PM} --- the weekly report: everything found that week, grouped by type, with a spreadsheet you can open in Excel or on your phone.
  \item \textbf{Weekends} --- rest. Updates return on Monday.
\end{itemize}

In a group, only group admins can turn alerts on or off. In a private chat with the bot, anyone can turn them on for themselves.

\section{Ask the assistant}

\cmd{.ask} answers any question, on any subject. Questions about current events are answered from today's web, with the sources listed. It is especially good at career help: CVs, cover letters, interviews, application essays and public-health career paths. Ask it to find something, and it runs a real search.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.ask} \textit{your question} & Ask anything \\
\cmd{.ask how do I write a cover letter for a WASH officer job} & Career and application help \\
\cmd{.ask what does a health records officer do} & Explanations in plain language \\
\cmd{.ask what is the current situation of public health in Kenya} & Current events and news: answered from today's web, with sources \\
\cmd{.ask find me nutrition internships in Kisumu} & Searches for real listings \\
\end{commands}

\begin{tip}
The assistant never makes up job adverts or links: listings always come from a real search. For your own health, it gives general information only. Please see a clinician for personal care.
\end{tip}

\section{Your CV and applications}

Chat with the bot in a \textbf{private chat}, in your own words. It builds your CV, finds opportunities that suit you and prepares each application: a CV tailored to the advert, a cover letter, and the email or portal steps. CVs and applications are always handled privately; in a group, these commands send you a private message instead.

\begin{commands}
\textit{Build me a CV for a data officer role} & Just ask. The bot asks for anything it still needs \\
\textit{(send your CV as a PDF or Word file)} & The bot reads it and fills in your profile \\
\textit{Find nutrition internships and apply to the ones I qualify for} & Searches, ranks by fit and prepares the applications \\
\cmd{.cv} / \cmd{.cv} \textit{role} & Build your CV (PDF and Word) for a kind of role \\
\cmd{.cv classic} \textit{role} / \cmd{.cv compact} \textit{role} & The same, in another design (\textit{modern} is the default) \\
\cmd{.profile} & See your saved profile; \cmd{.profile} \textit{change} to update it \\
\cmd{.apply 2} & Prepare an application for result 2 of your last search \\
\cmd{.applications} & Your applications and where each one stands \\
\cmd{sent 3} & Tell the bot you have sent application 3 \\
\cmd{submit 3} then \cmd{confirm 3} & For a simple online form: the bot fills it and shows you a picture; it is sent only after you reply \cmd{confirm 3} \\
\cmd{delete my data} or \cmd{.deletemydata} & Delete your profile, CVs and application history (you are asked to confirm) \\
\end{commands}

\begin{tip}
\textbf{Honest by design.} Your CV uses only what you told the bot: it never adds jobs, skills, grades or numbers you do not have. Applications by email are prepared for \textbf{you} to send from your own email (the bot gives you a ready email and a one-tap link). The bot never applies for you without your approval and never asks for passwords.
\end{tip}

\textbf{Your privacy.} Before saving anything, the bot asks your permission. Only you can see your profile and applications. They are deleted after 12 months without use, or at any time when you send \cmd{delete my data}.

\section{For group admins}

These work for the group's admins. The bot must be an admin of the group too.

\subsection{Managing the group}

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.groupinfo} & The group's details and settings (anyone) \\
\cmd{.add 2547XXXXXXXX} & Add someone (number with country code, no +) \\
\cmd{.kick @member} & Remove someone \\
\cmd{.promote @member} / \cmd{.demote @member} & Make or unmake an admin \\
\cmd{.close} / \cmd{.open} & Only admins can post / everyone can post \\
\cmd{.lock} / \cmd{.unlock} & Only admins / everyone can edit group info \\
\cmd{.welcome on} / \cmd{.welcome off} & Greet new members automatically \\
\cmd{.welcome set} \textit{message} & Your own greeting: \cmd{\{name\}} tags the newcomer, \cmd{\{group\}} is the group's name \\
\cmd{.welcome test} & Preview the greeting \\
\end{commands}

\subsection{Keeping the group clean}

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.mod} & Overview of the group's rules \\
\cmd{.mod on} & Recommended protection: delete and warn for invite links, adverts and spam \\
\cmd{.mod strict} & The same, and remove members who reach the warning limit \\
\cmd{.mod off} & Switch every rule off \\
\cmd{.mod test} \textit{message} & See what a message would trigger, without deleting anything \\
\cmd{.mod copy} & Copy these rules to the other groups of the community \\
\cmd{.antilink on} & Delete links to other WhatsApp groups and channels (job links still work); \cmd{.antilink on all} for every link \\
\cmd{.antilink allow who.int} & Always allow links to a website \\
\cmd{.antipromo on} & Delete adverts, sales pitches and money schemes \\
\cmd{.antispam on} & Delete flooding and the same message posted again and again \\
\cmd{.badword add} \textit{word} & Ban a word or phrase; \cmd{.badword on} to enforce, \cmd{.badword list} to see them \\
\cmd{.silence @member 2h} & Delete everything a member posts for a while (30m, 2h, 1d) \\
\cmd{.unsilence @member} / \cmd{.silenced} & Lift a silence / see who is silenced \\
\cmd{.warn @member} \textit{reason} & Give a warning \\
\cmd{.warnings @member} / \cmd{.resetwarns @member} & See / clear a member's warnings \\
\cmd{.warnlimit 3} & Warnings before removal (for \cmd{remove} rules) \\
\end{commands}

Each rule can be set to \cmd{warn} (warn only), \cmd{on} (delete and warn) or \cmd{remove} (delete, warn and remove at the warning limit). Group admins are never moderated.

\section{For platform administrators}

Only the numbers set up as administrators of the bot can use these.

\begin{commands}
\cmd{.scan} & Check every opportunity website right now and report what was found \\
\cmd{.pushall} & Send everything waiting into this chat at once, as a list and a spreadsheet \\
\cmd{.alerts interval 3-5} & How often opportunity cards go out (here every 3 to 5 minutes) \\
\cmd{.alerts pause} / \cmd{.alerts resume} & Pause or resume updates in every chat \\
\end{commands}

\cmd{.pushall} sends every opportunity that is still open and has not yet been sent to a chat, including ones found earlier in the week. Expired listings and repeats of the same job from other websites are left out, and nothing goes to the same chat twice.
